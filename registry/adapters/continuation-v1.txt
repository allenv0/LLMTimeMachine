# Continuation-style wrapper for base (pre-instruction) language models.

This adapter exists because GPT-2 XL and GPT-J predate chat interfaces. They are trained to continue text, not to follow instructions.

Template (`continuation-v1`):

```
Task: {raw_prompt}

Answer:
```

Rules:

- `raw_prompt` is inserted verbatim between `Task: ` and the blank line.
- No system message, no few-shot examples, no chain-of-thought nudge, no role tags.
- The trailing `Answer:` cue is the only structure; it is shown in the audit drawer as `prepared_text`.
- `was_truncated` is always false; over-length prompts raise `InputUnsupportedError`.
